Clear Hour

An open forecaster that ranks the hours of tomorrow by how breathable the air is in Islamabad / Rawalpindi.

Built with PriorLabs-TabPFN

TabPFN v2 needs roughly 1,000–2,500 training hours (about 6–15 weeks of hourly data) before it matches, and then edges, a one-line hour-of-day bias baseline on hour ranking. Below that it is worse and high-variance.

Mean gap in median within-day ranking ρ, TabPFN − B2: −0.12 at 100 h, −0.05 at 250 h, −0.12 at 500 h, +0.01 at 1,000 h, +0.07 at 2,500 h, +0.11 at 5,000 h. The edge is consistent across folds only at 5,000 h (4/4 folds; 3/5 at 1,000 and 2,500 h). Six rolling-origin folds, not the spent TEST month.
