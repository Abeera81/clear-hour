## Inputs and caveats

- Inputs are LIVE forecasts from the Open-Meteo air-quality and weather forecast endpoints (not the archives); the evaluation used archive inputs, so live skill is expected to be worse.
- Ground truth is an uncalibrated low-cost AirGradient sensor (OpenAQ 4554236; CC BY 4.0, The Urban Unit), not a reference monitor.
- On the held-out TEST month the exact cleanest-window hit rate (both hours inside the actual cleanest 6) was 37% for TabPFN v2 (30% raw CAMS, 43% B2).
- Feature coverage: all 26 features built from the forecast endpoints; none substituted from archives. Fill/null report: none. Forecast CAMS grid point 33.6, 73.100006.
- Known differences vs training: training CAMS/weather were archive values; here CAMS comes from the forecast endpoint (`cams_pm2_5_roll24` and lags use the 3 past days + forecast of the same endpoint), weather from api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast (`boundary_layer_height` is a model forecast; in training it was archive).
- Training data: monitor hours up to 2026-10-10 22:00:00 (11693 rows); B2 bias offsets fitted on all of it; TabPFN uses the most recent 10000.
